# Code of Conduct

Kavrune welcomes contributors and users with different backgrounds and levels of experience. This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions and other spaces maintained by the project.

Treat people with respect. Discuss behavior and technical evidence, give useful feedback, and accept corrections. Make participation possible for people using assistive technology and for contributors who are learning.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexual harassment, personal attacks, deliberate disruption and publication of another person's private information are unacceptable. Do not pressure anyone to expose personal data or run destructive tests on their equipment.

If a problem occurs, use a private conduct contact published by the repository maintainer. No contact address is assumed in this checkout. If none is listed, ask a maintainer to establish a private channel without exposing the incident or the people involved publicly. For software vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers will consider reports in context and limit sharing to what is needed to address them. Responses may include a request to stop, removal of content, a temporary participation restriction or removal from project spaces. Maintainers should explain decisions when appropriate and provide a way to request reconsideration through the same private channel. A maintainer involved in a report should defer handling when another maintainer is available.
